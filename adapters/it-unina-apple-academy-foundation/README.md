# Federico II Foundation Program

This source indexes the Foundation Program notices issued by the Università degli Studi di Napoli Federico II on its Apple Developer Academy notice page. Open the university page for the original call, eligibility and application instructions. The source is the university and does not represent Apple employment or claim separate evidence of Apple funding. [Official university notice page](https://www.unina.it/it/servizi-e-opportunita/opportunita-e-iniziative/apple-developer-academy)

The supported inventory is the current complete Foundation intake with cohorts I–IV. The collector verifies both current groups, original notice link identities, publication dates, cohort numbers and candidate cutoff dates from the visible HTML. It excludes unrelated Academy tracks, old undated editions, applicant results and commission documents. It does not open PDFs, attachments, application forms or Apple websites. Stable IDs pair the academic intake start year with the publisher's cohort marker. Each two-cohort call shares its own stated allocation. The 120 places for cohorts III and IV apply to the pair. Eligibility, venue and funding are unspecified in the indexed records.

The university states the real candidate cutoff date and 09:00 for each member of the current intake. The notice text supplies no timezone. The adapter stores the candidate date only and states the published clock and unspecified timezone in the original short summary. A date remains eligible through its latest end worldwide. A future printed date alone leaves current availability unknown. Successful publication filters dates which have passed, including previously published records. It atomically publishes an empty snapshot after a valid complete collection when all four dates have expired. A retrieval, parse or write failure preserves the last-good data and success time.

University robots permission was verified against the original Academy entry. The exact canonical cookie and privacy notices were subsequently reviewed through bounded GitHub Actions. The cookie page addresses technical cookies and the privacy page describes university handling of personal data. The reviewed texts contained no applicable automation or reuse clause. The output uses only programme facts, short original summaries and official links. The adapter fetches fresh robots and the literal Academy HTML only. It follows no redirects and retains a held or unresolved attempt. The publisher's verified timing, backoff and ownership state carries forward before each hosted collection. The physical completion gap is at least 12.2 seconds with at most five starts in any rolling minute. There is one inventory page and no continuation page. Each received 429 immediately and durably doubles the current physical and inventory gaps and preserves the strongest embargo before body reading. The exact active state and last acknowledged state govern recovery. No verified incremental boundary is available, so collection revalidates the entire supported Foundation inventory.

The permanent workflow runs once daily at 16:46 UTC and after authorized main merge. It writes only this source's atomic data/metadata pair.
