# CERN Summer Studentship

This source lists CERN's general Summer Student Programme and its computing-focused openlab Summer Student Programme. It links each record to its official careers page. CERN repeats the same vacancy under multiple disciplines and has reused URL slugs that mention an older year. The adapter uses the official reference and matching title to determine the cycle, collapses identical cards and stops on conflicts or unreviewed detail URLs. [Publisher inventory](https://careers.cern/programmes/summer-studentship/)

The 2027 calls both close January 21 at 23:59 Geneva time. The general call lists June 14 as its ideal start and openlab lists June 21. Both careers pages specify a net allowance of CHF 94 per calendar day, CERN insurance subject to the stated exceptions and possible travel reimbursement depending on circumstances. Candidates must remain enrolled at bachelor's or master's level throughout the placement, have completed six semesters by the start of summer, have good English and meet CERN's restrictions on prior work and programme participation. The general call includes member and non-member states. Openlab separately confirms applicants of all nationalities in its 2027 FAQ. The careers cards' filter labels are unsuitable for inferring citizenship eligibility, so the published records leave individual country codes unspecified. [General 2027 call](https://careers.cern/jobs/cern-summer-student-programme-2026-member-and-non-member-state/) · [Openlab 2027 call](https://careers.cern/jobs/cern-openlab-summer-student-programme-2026/) · [CERN openlab FAQ](https://openlab.cern/faqs-summer-student-programme/)

The reviewed publisher responses permit the scoped careers paths under the captured robots policy. The publisher's careers privacy page links privacy notices and audiovisual terms. The adapter uses plain factual fields and original summaries and links to the publisher. Application forms, external recruitment systems, site assets and other CERN career programmes are outside the collection frontier. [Publisher privacy and media notices](https://careers.cern/privacy-policy/) · [Programme capture](https://github.com/YouthOpps/ai-workspace/actions/runs/44490215147) · [Detail capture](https://github.com/YouthOpps/ai-workspace/actions/runs/44492847737)

A refresh requests robots, one complete inventory page and both reviewed details. The publisher has no verified incremental signal for this scope. The adapter requires original state continuity across research, native QA and publication. It retains the physical and inventory pacing floors, holds and embargoes and checks current access before each request. A failed response leaves the last good published dataset intact.

The candidate production workflow uses the source's 08:41 UTC slot. Upon separately reviewed activation, it runs `python -B adapters/cern-summer-studentships/adapter.py --publish` and atomically updates the source's dataset and metadata, removing records after their verified Geneva cutoff. The sole native nonpublishing test runs through the reviewed GitHub Actions scope using `python -B adapters/cern-summer-studentships/test_adapter.py`. Production requires the exact accepted native state. [Integration issue](https://github.com/YouthOpps/data-pipeline/issues/459)
