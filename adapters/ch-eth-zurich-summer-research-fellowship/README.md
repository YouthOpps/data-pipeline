# ETH Zürich Student Summer Research Fellowship

This adapter indexes the annual fellowship cohort published by the ETH Zürich Department of Computer Science. It checks the two application windows for agreement, reads the official research dates, verifies the posted closing timezone and confirms the stipend and travel and visa coverage. Each cohort has one stable identifier based on the fellowship year. The official application page is the public record URL. The adapter does not query, sign in to, or submit an application through SiROP.

The application page gives the academic and English-language requirements. The department FAQ separately describes nationality eligibility and the exclusion of current ETH students. The adapter does not infer either condition from the application page or map citizenship restrictions into country codes. Applicants can check the full conditions on the official programme and FAQ pages.

- [Application page](https://inf.ethz.ch/studies/summer-research-fellowship/how-to-apply.html)
- [Programme](https://inf.ethz.ch/studies/summer-research-fellowship.html)
- [FAQ](https://inf.ethz.ch/studies/summer-research-fellowship/faqs.html)

The adapter requests the publisher robots route and the single canonical application page. The [first hosted capture](https://github.com/YouthOpps/ai-workspace/actions/runs/44492347248) returned an unavailable robots file and the public application page. The adapter honors explicit rules, applicable indexing directives and publisher restrictions and preserves the acknowledged request history. The university's [copyright statement](https://ethz.ch/en/footer/disclaimer-copyright.html) restricts storing copies of pages or their parts on other servers and permits linking and attributed citation. The [departmental notice](https://inf.ethz.ch/footer/disclaimer-copyright.html) is linked from the application page. Publication and verification keep original factual summaries, attributed links and pacing state. The adapter does not export publisher HTML, images or contact details.

A daily full refresh at 18:41 UTC reconciles the single annual record. There is no published incremental inventory. Publication removes expired records at the exact official time following complete successful collection and keeps the previous dataset on failure. GitHub Actions owns source request history and exclusive collection. The sole nonpublishing hosted test runs `python -B adapters/ch-eth-zurich-summer-research-fellowship/test_adapter.py` under the reviewed native Action. Production uses `python -B adapters/ch-eth-zurich-summer-research-fellowship/adapter.py --publish` and the separately authorized first-run handoff.
